Current Year Tax Scheme & Policy Overview

Key Tax Schemes and Benefits (2024-2025 Filing Year):

  • **New Regime (Simplified):** Lower slab rates, default option. Requires taxpayers to forgo most common deductions and exemptions.
  • **Old Regime (Traditional):** Standard rates, but allows claiming a wider range of deductions (e.g., standard deduction, investments under 80C, health insurance under 80D). Must be explicitly chosen.
  • **Deduction Highlights:** Overview of common exemptions under sections like 80C (max 1.5L), 80D (Health Insurance), and home loan interest benefits.
  • Note: The optimal regime depends heavily on your personal investments and financial situation.
